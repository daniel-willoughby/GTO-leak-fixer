PotKing Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PotKing is a poker strategy trainer. It is designed to work fully offline. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. By using PotKing you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • PotKing works without an account. Your training data stays on your device.
  • Creating an account (optional) stores your email, chosen username, and game progress so it can sync across your devices and appear on leaderboards.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use advertising trackers.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of its cloud data from inside the app at any time.

Information stored on your device

As you drill, PotKing records your decisions, streaks, lesson progress, spaced-repetition queue, and cosmetic preferences locally on your device (using standard on-device storage). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to create an account and sync. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Information stored when you create an account

Account creation is optional and used only to back up and sync your progress. If you sign up, we store:

  • Email address — used to sign you in and secure your account.
  • Username / display name — shown on leaderboards and in friend duels. This is chosen by you and is public.
  • Game progress and cosmetics — your Poker Points activity, owned and equipped cosmetic items, daily-challenge scores, and head-to-head duel records.

This data is stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts our database and handles authentication on our behalf. Access is protected by row-level security so you can only modify your own records. See Supabase's privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No real-money gambling or wagering. Poker Points are an in-app score earned by playing and have no cash value.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: open Account in the app and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your authentication record and all associated cloud data (profile, username, scores, and duels). This action cannot be undone. Any progress stored locally on a device can be cleared by uninstalling the app on that device.

Children

PotKing is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
